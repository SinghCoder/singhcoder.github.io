What I'm Doing Now

Qordinate

I'm building Qordinate - a coordination assistant that helps people and their agents work together seamlessly, without the endless back-and-forth.

I've spent the past few years thinking about how humans and AI can coordinate better - not just communicate. With Qordinate, we're making that real by bringing autonomous, context-aware agents into the apps people already use.

Latest: Qordinate now works on WhatsApp and inside Arattai (download vCard).
