# About Me

I'm Harpinder Jot Singh, an AI/ML engineer and entrepreneur focused on building coordination tools that help humans and AI work together seamlessly.

## What I'm Building

I'm currently building **Qordinate** - a coordination assistant that helps people and their agents work together seamlessly, without the endless back-and-forth. I've spent the past few years thinking about how humans and AI can coordinate better - not just communicate. With Qordinate, we're making that real by bringing autonomous, context-aware agents into the apps people already use.

## My Background

Before starting Qordinate, I spent ~4.5 years at DevRev, helping take it from 0→1, working on workflows and the agent platform - one of its core foundations. I've worked across the full stack of AI systems, from data pipelines to user interfaces, and have experience building platforms that scale.

## Writing & Speaking

I write about the evolving agent ecosystem, interoperability between assistants, and what it means to build AI products that actually fit into people's lives. I've also spoken about these topics, including a recent podcast with Dheeraj Pandey (former CEO of Nutanix, current CEO of DevRev) on The Effortless Podcast.

## Education

I graduated from BITS Pilani with a degree in Computer Science, where I was actively involved in coding clubs, teaching assistantships, and various technical projects.
