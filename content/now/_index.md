# What I'm Doing Now

*Last updated: October 2025*

## Building Qordinate

I'm currently focused on building **Qordinate** - a coordination assistant that helps people and their agents work together seamlessly, without the endless back-and-forth.

### Recent Updates

- **Arattai Integration:** Qordinate now works inside Arattai! Users can download the contact card from [qordinate.ai/vcf](https://qordinate.ai/vcf) and DM it on Arattai to get started.
- **Agent Platform:** We're making autonomous, context-aware agents that fit into the apps people already use, rather than requiring new interfaces.

## The Vision

I've spent the past few years thinking about how humans and AI can coordinate better - not just communicate. The key insight is that coordination requires shared context, not just better chat interfaces. Qordinate brings this shared context to messaging apps people already use.

## What's Next

- Expanding integrations with more messaging platforms
- Improving agent reasoning and context awareness
- Building tools for developers to create their own coordination agents
